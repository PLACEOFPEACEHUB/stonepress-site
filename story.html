Our story

Stonepress is being built by STRIPP'D JUICE LLC, a New Jersey company formed in 2021 and rooted in Riverton.

The company comes out of Riverton's martial arts community. Its sister business runs an academy where the day starts early and the standard is simple: show up, do the work, and take care of your body between sessions. Anyone who trains knows the gap in that standard. The food and drink within reach of most gyms is built on syrups, powders, and labels nobody can pronounce.

Stonepress is the food side of the standard. Real produce, pressed fresh. Smoothies built on nut milk and whole fruit instead of mixes. Shots that taste like what is in them. Bowls that carry a training day. And labels that say exactly what the lab found, because every nutrition value we publish will come from analysis of the finished drink.

The name carries the method. Stone fruit and stone ground patience on one side, pressure and permanence on the other. A press is an honest machine: what goes in is what comes out. That is the whole business idea.

Riverton opens first, at 600 Main Street. A Philadelphia home at 3550 North 9th Street follows. Both are in preparation now, and this site will carry real opening dates the moment they exist. Until then, the menu preview shows what is being built, and the wholesale page shows how stores, gyms, and offices can be part of it.
